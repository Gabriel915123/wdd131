My Country

Ecuador is a small but remarkably diverse country in South America, straddling the equator from which it takes its name. Despite covering less land than many of its neighbors, it holds four distinct regions — the Pacific coast, the Andean highlands, the Amazon rainforest, and the Galápagos Islands — each with its own climate and wildlife. The Galápagos alone inspired Charles Darwin's theory of evolution and remain home to species found nowhere else on Earth. Quito, the capital, sits high in the Andes and is one of the best-preserved historic centers in Latin America. Ecuador's currency is the US dollar, and its culture blends Indigenous, Spanish, and Afro-Ecuadorian influences into a rich national identity.
