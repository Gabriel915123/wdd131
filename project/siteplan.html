Eternal Path

Website Planning Document — WDD 131

1. Site Name

Eternal Path

This name represents a website exploring the Plan of Salvation as taught by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints — the doctrine that life is a purposeful, continuing journey through premortal life, mortality, death, and resurrection, rather than a single isolated event. "Path" reflects the sense of ongoing progression central to this teaching.

Optional domain availability: eternal-path.org

2. Site Purpose

The site provides a clear, organized overview of the Plan of Salvation for visitors who are curious about Latter-day Saint beliefs on life before birth, the purpose of mortality, and what happens after death. Each major stage of the plan — premortal life, earth life, the spirit world, resurrection and judgment, and the degrees of glory — is presented on its own page with supporting scripture references and quotes from Church leaders.

3. Scenarios

Sample questions a visitor might bring to this site:

  • What happens to me after I die, according to Latter-day Saint teachings?
  • Where can I find scriptures that support the doctrine of a premortal life?
  • What are the three degrees of glory, and how do they differ from one another?

4. Color Schema

This document itself uses the color schema described below.

  • Deep Blue #1B3A5C — headers, footer background, and main headings
  • Gold #C9A227 — accents, links, borders, and section dividers
  • Paper White #FAF8F3 — page background
  • Ink #222222 — body text

5. Typography

This document uses the fonts described below.

  • Playfair Display (serif) — used for the site title and all headings, giving the page a formal, reverent tone.
  • Nunito Sans (sans-serif) — used for body text, lists, and captions, keeping longer passages easy to read.
