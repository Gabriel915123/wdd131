Welcome to the Plan of Salvation

Where did I come from? Why am I here? What happens after I die? This topic walks through the answers taught by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, known as the Plan of Salvation — a framework that gives purpose to premortal life, mortality, and the life that continues after death.

What Is the Plan of Salvation?

Latter-day Saints believe that life did not begin at birth and does not end at death. Before this life, we lived as spirit children of Heavenly Father. We chose to come to earth to receive a body, exercise our agency, and learn through experience. Because none of us are perfect, Jesus Christ's Atonement makes it possible to overcome mistakes and death itself. After this life, every person will be resurrected and will stand before God to be judged according to how they lived.

This plan does not stay abstract — it is organized into five major stages, each explored in more depth on the Stages page.
