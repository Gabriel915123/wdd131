Scriptures & Resources

These scripture references support the doctrine covered on the Stages page. Reading them directly is the best way to study this topic further.

Key References

  • Abraham 3:22-23 Describes the premortal existence of spirits before the earth was created.
  • 2 Nephi 2:25 Teaches that "men are, that they might have joy" — the purpose of mortal life.
  • Alma 34:32 Describes mortal life as "the time for men to prepare to meet God."
  • Alma 40:11-14 Explains the condition of the spirit after death, before the resurrection.
  • Doctrine and Covenants 138 Records a vision of the spirit world and the work of teaching the gospel there.
  • Alma 11:43-44 Teaches that the resurrection restores every person's body to a perfect frame.
  • Doctrine and Covenants 76 Describes the three degrees of glory given to all who are resurrected.
